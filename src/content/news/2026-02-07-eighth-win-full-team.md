# Eight Wins! Incredible Comeback as QPR Fight Back to Beat Sonics 2-1

**Final Score: QPR 2-1 PCFC Sonics**

In a match that showcased everything this team is about, QPR came from behind to secure their eighth victory of the season with a stunning 2-1 comeback win against PCFC Sonics at Percy Perry Stadium South. For the first time in weeks, the team had everyone available - and they showed exactly why they've become a team that never gives up!

## A Tactical First Half

The opening 30 minutes was a closely contested affair with both teams creating opportunities. The defense, with excellent support from **Travis C** blocking chances, managed to keep a clean sheet going into the break. QPR had some near chances but couldn't convert as the half ended 0-0.

## Going Behind - But Never Giving Up

QPR started the second half strongly, looking the more likely team to score. However, football can be cruel - the Sonics scored against the run of play to take a 1-0 lead.

**But this is a team with heart.** There was no panic, no heads dropping. Instead, the players immediately pushed forward with renewed determination. This is the character that has transformed QPR's season - the belief that no matter what happens, they will fight back.

## Ethan's Brilliant Equalizer

The comeback began with **Ethan G** who produced a brilliant shot following a corner kick. A moment of quality that brought the game level at 1-1 and lifted the entire team. The momentum had shifted.

## Henry Completes the Comeback

After coming close twice, QPR won a penalty when a defender blocked the ball with his hand in the box. **Henry K** - who was having one of his best games of the season - stepped up with ice in his veins and calmly converted to complete the comeback. 2-1 QPR!

## Outstanding Performances

**Henry K** was simply outstanding today - one of the best players on the pitch. His work rate, positioning, and composure for the penalty were exceptional.

Also delivering strong performances were:
- **Owen H** - Solid throughout the match
- **Rhodri W** - Reliable as always at the back
- **Yejun K** - Great contribution in midfield
- **Niko D** - Tireless work across the pitch

## Milestone: 8 Wins, 7 Losses - The Ultimate Comeback Season

This eighth win brings the season record to 8 wins and 7 losses - the first time this season we've had more wins than losses!

And what better way to reach this milestone than with a comeback victory? Just like today's match, this entire season has been about fighting back. From heavy early-season defeats to now having a winning record - this team embodies the comeback spirit in everything they do.

## Full Team, Full Strength

Having the full squad available made a difference today. The depth and options allowed for better rotation and kept everyone fresh throughout the game.

## Looking Ahead

Next up is an away trip to face CMFSC Blackburn Rovers on February 21st. With momentum building and confidence high, QPR will be looking to make it nine wins!

**Man of the Match: Henry K** - An exceptional all-round performance capped with the composed penalty that sealed the comeback.
