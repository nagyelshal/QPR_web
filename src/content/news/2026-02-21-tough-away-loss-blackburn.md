# Tough Away Day as QPR Fall 0-3 to Blackburn Rovers

**Final Score: CMFSC Blackburn Rovers 3-0 QPR**

QPR travelled to Miller Park East knowing this would be a tough test, and so it proved. Against a strong and physical Blackburn Rovers side, QPR went down 0-3 in a match where the scoreline didn't fully reflect the team's effort.

## A Hard-Fought Battle

This was a difficult game from the start. Blackburn are a tough team and they made life uncomfortable throughout. **Henry K** started in goal for the first half, with **Andrew N** taking over between the posts for the second.

The team never stopped trying. QPR created a number of chances throughout the match but couldn't find the finishing touch to get back into the game. On another day, some of those opportunities go in and it's a very different story.

## Missed Opportunities

The most frustrating aspect was the volume of chances created without reward. The team pushed hard to get back into the game and showed the right attitude - but football doesn't always reward effort. The missed chances were the difference between a tough loss and what could have been a competitive match.

## Unsportsmanlike Behaviour

It's worth noting that several unsportsmanlike behaviours from the opposition were observed during the game - something unusual at this level. Despite the provocation, QPR's players kept their composure and continued to play the right way.

## Not a Bad Performance

While 0-3 looks harsh on paper, this was not a bad performance. The effort, the desire to compete, and the willingness to keep pushing forward were all there. Sometimes in football you come up against a team on their day and the chances don't fall your way.

## Season Record: 8 Wins, 8 Losses

The record now stands level at 8 wins and 8 losses. A setback, but this team has shown time and again that they respond to adversity. The character built through this season's comebacks will serve them well.

## Looking Ahead

Next up is another away trip to face CMFSC Middlesbrough on February 28th - a team QPR beat 5-2 back in November. Time to bounce back!
